## Books to Read

### Must Read
1. **The Scrum Guide** (Schwaber & Sutherland)
   - Free PDF available
   - Start here

2. **Scrum Mastery: From Good to Great Servant-Leadership** by Geoff Watts
   - Best for SM role
   - Practical advice

3. **Inspired: How to Create Products Customers Love** by Marty Cagan
   - Product management bible
   - Real-world examples

### Recommended
4. **Essential Kanban Condensed** by David J. Anderson & Andy Carmichael
   - Short, practical
   - Easy to digest

5. **Lean Software Development** by Tom Poppendieck
   - Lean principles
   - Kanban foundations

6. **The Lean Product Playbook** by Dan Olsen
   - Product development
   - Practical frameworks

## Study Schedule Recommendation

### Month 1: Foundation
- Week 1: Read Agile Manifesto + Scrum Guide (free)
- Week 2: Kanban Guide + Basics
- Week 3: Scrum.org free resources
- Week 4: Set up Jira, practice board creation

### Month 2: Deep Dive
- Week 1-2: Take Scrum course (Coursera/Udemy)
- Week 3-4: Practice exercises on Jira

### Month 3: Mastery
- Week 1-2: Advanced topics, SAFe intro
- Week 3-4: Certification exam prep

### Month 4+: Certification & Beyond
- Take PSM I exam
- Apply to real projects
- Continuous learning
